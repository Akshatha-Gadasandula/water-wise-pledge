🌊 Water Conservation Tips Portal

A simple and interactive web platform designed to promote awareness about water conservation, aligned with SDG 6 – Clean Water and Sanitation.

🌟 Overview

The Water Conservation Tips Portal educates users on practical and effective methods to save water. It encourages small daily actions that collectively make a big environmental impact.

✨ Features

📘 Static Informational Pages — Easy-to-read content explaining why water conservation matters.

📝 Water Conservation Pledge — Users take a pledge or participate in a small quiz to learn responsible habits.

💧 Tip of the Day — Displays a new actionable water-saving tip every day.

🤖 AI-Powered Reminders — (Optional add-on) Generates personalized daily conservation reminders to encourage consistent habits.

🎯 Goal

To create a digital awareness tool that spreads knowledge, inspires responsible usage, and supports a sustainable future.
